# Create on the website

## Generate

1. Open the [home page](/), enter your product's URL (for example `linear.app`) or a public GitHub repository URL, and press **Create**.
2. Skeleton Motion reads the page, designs up to three animations about what the product does, and opens them in the **Display Room**. This usually takes under a minute.

In the Display Room you can switch scenes and animations, toggle light and dark, press **Copy code**, or **Download ZIP**.

## Free generations and your own AI key

Each browser gets **10 free generations**. The line under the input shows how many are left.

After that, add your own AI key with **Use your own AI key**:

| Provider | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| Gemini | Your Google AI Studio API key. The model is optional. |
| OpenAI-compatible (OpenAI, DeepSeek, Qwen, OpenRouter, ...) | Your key, the model name, and the provider's API URL (https only). |

Your key is checked with the provider, then stored **encrypted**. It is never shown again or logged. Generations use it until you press **Remove key** under **Manage key**.

## Gallery

The [Gallery](/gallery) has two tabs:

- **My generations**: everything made in this browser, no account needed. Use **Public / Private** on a card to show or hide it in the shared gallery.
- **All**: public generations from everyone.

New generations are **public**, and cards show the page address. Private generations are not listed, but anyone with their link can still open them.

To use your gallery in another browser, press **Copy gallery link** and open it there. Keep that link private: it opens your gallery and lets its holder generate with a key you saved (they cannot see the key).
