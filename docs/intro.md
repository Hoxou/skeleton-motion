# Overview

Skeleton Motion designs short, looping animations of what your product does, styled with your product's own colors and type.

1. **Paste your product's URL** (or a public GitHub repository) on the [home page](/) and press **Create**.
2. **Watch the result** in the Display Room: each animation in every frame shape, in light and dark.
3. **Take it with you**: press **Copy code** for a React or HTML component, or **Download ZIP** for the SVG files.

Every animation is a transparent, self-contained SVG that loops and scales to its box. The copied components show a still frame to visitors who prefer reduced motion.
